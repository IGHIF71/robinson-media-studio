Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

Robinson Media Studio is a content publishing workflow used to prepare and publish podcast promotional videos. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users authorize the app and use the workflow.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information:

  • TikTok OAuth authorization data
  • TikTok access tokens and refresh tokens
  • TikTok user identifiers such as open_id
  • Podcast audio files
  • Generated video files
  • Episode titles, descriptions, captions, and hashtags
  • Publishing status, post results, and related workflow logs

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate the podcast publishing workflow, generate promotional video content, and upload or publish content to accounts authorized by the user.

The app does not sell user data. The app does not post content to TikTok without authorization from the account owner or approved workflow user.

Third-Party Services

The workflow may use third-party services such as TikTok, Make.com, file storage providers, podcast hosting platforms, and video rendering tools to process and publish content.

User Control

Users may revoke TikTok access at any time through their TikTok account settings or by contacting us.
