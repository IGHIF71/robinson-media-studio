Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

What creative media services do you offer?

We create promotional flyers, posters, social media graphics, GIFs, motion content, short commercials, and coordinated digital campaign assets for businesses and nonprofits.

Can you design flyers for events and nonprofit campaigns?

Yes. We can create event and promotional designs using your branding, campaign details, and intended distribution formats.

Do you produce video commercials and social media ads?

Yes. We can develop short-form promotional video content and digital ads. Production methods, footage, voiceover, editing, and platform formats are defined by the project scope.

Can you use our existing logo, colors, and brand guidelines?

Yes. We work with supplied logos, brand files, and guidelines when available. If those assets are missing, we can discuss creating or refining the needed creative direction.

How much does a flyer or commercial cost?

Creative projects are quoted based on format, complexity, number of deliverables, video requirements, revisions, and usage needs. You can request a one-time project without subscribing to a monthly plan.

How many design revisions are included?

Revision rounds and deliverables are documented in your proposal before work begins, so both sides understand the scope.

Can one campaign be adapted for multiple social platforms?

Yes. We can create coordinated variations for different placements, aspect ratios, and messaging needs. Final platform formats depend on the approved scope.

Can you schedule and publish the content you create?

Yes, content distribution and Make.com publishing workflows can be arranged as additional services where supported by platform integrations and permissions.
