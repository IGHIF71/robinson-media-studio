Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

What digital marketing services do you provide?

We offer Google Ads management, search engine optimization, landing-page work, campaign creative, conversion tracking, and performance reporting, depending on the selected package and project scope.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO improves the ability of your website to earn visibility in unpaid search results over time. Google Ads can place paid advertisements in relevant searches while campaigns are active, subject to bidding and platform policies.

Do you also optimize websites for AI search and answer engines?

Yes. Our AI Search Optimization services focus on useful answer-focused content, crawlability, structured information, accurate business details, and monitoring where reporting is available. AI mentions and citations cannot be guaranteed.

How much does digital marketing management cost?

Our Essentials managed package is listed at $1,500 per month and Growth at $2,500 per month, with separate initialization fees. The appropriate package depends on your services and needs; one-time work may be quoted separately.

Is my advertising budget included in the management fee?

No. Ad spend and applicable third-party platform costs are separate unless a written proposal explicitly includes them.

How soon will SEO or advertising generate leads?

Results vary with competition, budget, offer quality, tracking, and website readiness. Paid campaigns can begin collecting performance data after launch, while organic search typically requires sustained work and time.

Can you help a business attract local customers in Atlanta?

We can target relevant Atlanta-area searches with location-conscious website content, local SEO foundations, and appropriate advertising campaigns. Local search visibility depends on multiple factors.

How will I know whether marketing is working?

We focus on actionable measures such as qualified inquiries, conversions, campaign costs, and relevant search metrics. Reporting scope and tools are agreed upon for each engagement.
